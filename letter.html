Dear birthday boy,

Happy birthday, my love! I hope this new chapter of your life brings you good health, peace of mind, and lots of happiness.
May all the things you've been wishing for come true, and I hope you do really well in your exams too. I know you can do your best, and your baby believes in you so much.
I hope you'll understand just how much your baby loves you. And I hope you'll always be caring son to your parents. Honestly, you already are, so I don't even need to remind you about that. Most of all, I hope your days are filled with happy moments and beautiful things. I just want to see you happy, my love. That's all your baby wants. I love you so, so much, my birthday boy.

from me ♡
